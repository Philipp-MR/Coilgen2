# Martinos Center acknowledgment artwork

`martinos-logo.png` is the unchanged 505 × 86 PNG from the
[official Martinos Center website](https://www.martinos.org/):
[original asset](https://www.martinos.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/logo_v2.png),
retrieved 2026-10-09.

SHA-256: `2b7e2302add9e453ec57f9afea3ef7f8dc7623558745655329a3cadc4f3617db`.

The GUI displays the mark for Philipp Amrein's personal acknowledgment of the
Athinoula A. Martinos Center for Biomedical Imaging community. No institutional
endorsement, sponsorship, or ownership of CoilGen2 is represented.

This is third-party institutional artwork, separate from any license for
CoilGen2 source code. No permission to reuse the logo is asserted here.
