# CoilGen2 browser workbench validation

3 November 2026 · Windows · Codex internal browser

This historical pre-fix audit covers the 19 retained gallery templates. The [repeat validation](FIXES.md) records the subsequent fixes. A later check of the archived shielded Gy data corrected the physical interpretation of the suppression norm.

**The retained matrix contains 44 browser-submitted jobs.** Forty-three completed and one queued sheet job was intentionally cancelled. The completed jobs comprised 29 full evaluations, seven preparations, four sheet solves, two contour runs and one routed run. All 29 full evaluations passed the independent sheet checks and failed both swept-conductor and fabrication review. No run recorded a construction release.

All 43 completed evidence records passed integrity verification, including 711 artifact entries. Across 3,939 stored arrays, no numeric array contained a nonfinite value. The independent B∥ sheet RMS error across the full evaluations ranged from 2.984% for Halbach Gx to 4.310% for the biplanar system XZ channel. The matrix distinguishes solver completion, the boundary reached, each engineering verdict and the recorded failure reasons.

## Coverage

I launched `Start CoilGen2 Browser.cmd` in local and team modes, bound the listeners to loopback, and used disposable workspaces. I opened all 19 retained gallery entries and submitted full evaluation for every standalone entry and every channel in both systems. The [run matrix](run-matrix.csv) identifies each submission, its revision, terminal state, engineering verdicts and evidence directory. Full evaluation includes sheet optimization, contour generation, routing, sweep and independent field validation.

| Gallery family | Full-evaluation coverage |
|---|---|
| Single gradient coils | Cylindrical, Halbach and biplanar Gx/Gy/Gz, plus actively shielded Gy |
| Single shim coils | Uniform Z0, Z2, XZ, YZ, X²−Y², XY and Conical Z2 |
| Systems | Three cylindrical gradient channels and all nine biplanar gradient/shim channels |

I also submitted the four earlier compute boundaries, cancelled a separate queued sheet job, imported a synthetic STL and the repository's real octagonal Halbach Gy remesh, and exercised model editing, channel management, project save/open, result selection, field plots and CAD export. The browser gallery is distinct from `projects/`. For example, the browser XYZ system produced support diameters of 160, 172 and 184 mm, while `projects/standard_cylindrical_3axis_gradient` specifies 130, 132 and 134 mm. The octagonal Halbach project also has separate input definitions. Importing its Gy surface exercised the browser's geometry path. The primitive-support scripts and general-example drivers have no direct browser launch action and were outside the end-to-end runs.

Its geometry hashes verified, but its project companion had the wrong model fingerprint. | Bind the filename and companion to the immutable source run. Compare project ID, revision and model fingerprint before including inputs. Exercise cross-project and same-project-after-edit exports. |

Each geometry setter builds a complete parameter tuple from the old configuration. | Apply cylinder edits atomically and refresh fields from the committed model. ## Other reproduced workflow defects

- **The batch ignores the displayed compute boundary.** With “Prepare model” selected, “Compute enabled channels” submitted Gx, Gy and Gz as `evaluated`. Single-channel Compute honored the selected boundary. The batch uses each channel's previously stored study setting. These three unintended full jobs occupied a single worker for about 9.7 minutes. Show per-channel boundaries explicitly or commit the selected boundary to the intended channels before queuing them. See `web/app.py` lines 357–367 and `application/session.py` lines 601–639.
- **Switching projects leaves stale verdicts and export options.** A new or newly loaded model retained the previous model's checks and geometry choices, even with an empty run selector. Historical job rows also lose their channel names once the active assembly changes. A result panel should clear or explicitly identify its source project, channel and revision before a user interprets a verdict.
- **Successful preparation is labeled “Checks failed”.** Cylindrical Gx run `14267f0e` completed preparation, but Results marked the sheet “Failed” although no sheet solve ran. Independently, completed progress bars rendered `aria-valuenow=1` against `aria-valuemax=100`. Use boundary-aware verdicts and a consistent progress scale.
- **“Cylinder length [mm]” shows a legacy mesher parameter.** The cylindrical Gy form displayed 212.5 mm at 16 axial divisions, while the actual support spanned 200 mm. The preset deliberately compensates for the legacy generator's `n / (n + 1)` span. Expose the physical end-to-end length or label the stored value and show its physical span. This is separate from edits being silently discarded.
- **Duplicate channel fails for both new and multichannel projects.** The existing document ID violates the assembly's independent-channel invariant. Channel selection and removal worked. The browser offers “Compute enabled channels” but no per-channel enable control. Save, undo/redo, copy and remove also lack accessible button names.
- **Advanced JSON rejects syntax errors well, but fails on a valid array.** `[]` left the dialog open without actionable feedback and logged `AttributeError: 'list' object has no attribute 'items'`. Cancel recovered the UI. Check the top-level JSON type before walking either document.
- **The narrow internal-browser layout clips engineering context.** At a measured viewport width of 595 px, the main pane needed 708 px, and the open drawer, field view and results competed for the same space. The project title and B0 chip were truncated. A collapsible results column or a narrow-view tab layout would keep the active model, run and plot readable together.

## Controls and numerical checks

- The synthetic plate reopened from its downloaded JSON alone in a separate workspace, with source-file access blocked. Four vertices and two triangles spanned 100 × 50 mm at z = 75 mm. Preparation completed on both the plate and the octagonal Gy remesh. The latter contained 7,626 vertices and 15,012 triangles and spanned 184 × 290 × 184 mm in the world frame.
- Invalid project JSON and zero B0 direction produced visible errors. A valid gradient edit survived undo/redo (20 → 25 → 20 → 25 mT/m). All six settings sections opened, the three conductor profile actions responded, and VTK orbit worked.
- A failed cylindrical sweep required selecting the DRAFT artifact and acknowledging the recorded failures before export in metres. The ZIP retained its DRAFT and inspection-only status. JSON and ZIP responses were HTTP 200 and matched the files on disk. The internal browser’s native ZIP download event timed out, so that event did not verify client-side receipt.
- Independent B∥ sheet error in the matrix is `100 × norm(B∥,sheet − B∥,target) / norm(B∥,target)` on the saved holdout grid. The 3,112-point cylindrical Gy check measured 3.109%. At the original 18.6343 A, the fabricated path had a 4.105% design-ROI residual while its sweep failed clearance, bend, self-intersection and support-penetration checks. Target extrema were ±0.400 mT at ±20 mm for 20 mT/m.
- The shielded Gy sheet achieved 4.057% independent target error. The initially reported 14.36 was the area-weighted conic norm scaled by 10⁶. Dividing by the square root of the 0.515678 m² sample area gives a physical sheet vector RMS of **19.999 µT** against the 20 µT ceiling. At the same 314 design vertices the fabricated winding gives **29.525 µT**. Independent post-route suppression remains unverified. A field-accurate sheet or a completed job is insufficient evidence of manufacturability.
- All five cylindrical second-order shims and Conical Z2 completed and passed independent sheet validation. Only XZ and YZ met their fabricated-field limits, and both still failed geometric checks. Conical Z2's independent sheet error was 3.125%, while its fabricated-path RMS was 0.577 µT against a 0.139 µT limit. Standalone biplanar Gx likewise reached a fabricated-field residual of 11.917%, despite 4.293% independent sheet error.

## Reproduction

- Start `Start CoilGen2 Browser.cmd --no-browser --host 127.0.0.1 --port <port> --data-root <empty-qa-directory>` and open the printed loopback URL in the internal browser. Add `--team --max-users 8 --password <test-password>` to exercise team sessions. Local and team cases used the same Windows launcher and Python 3.12.16 with NumPy 2.5.4, SciPy 1.18.1, CVXPY 1.9.3, trame 4.0.0, PyVista 0.49.2 and VTK 9.7.1.
- The accompanying isolated regression run passed 176 tests in 32.95 s. These tests supplement the real browser runs.
- For reproducibility in this checkout, SHA-256 of `src/coilgen/web/app.py` was `f3979f075049f11bfadb6f5f074cdc00e57050764ce1fd6bde3cec96c88f8c09`.
